# Returns and Refunds

Each store is run by an independent seller. This page sets the rules that apply to every order on Saloree. A seller may offer more generous terms on their store page, never less than the law gives you.

## 1. Right to withdraw (consumers in the EU/EEA)
If you are a consumer and order from a trader in the EU/EEA, you have the right to withdraw from the purchase **within 14 days after you receive the goods**, without giving a reason.
- To withdraw, use the **"Withdraw from contract"** function in your order in your account, or write to the seller or to **[[SUPPORT_EMAIL]]** with your order number. You will get a confirmation of receipt.
- Send the goods back within 14 days after you told us you withdraw.
- The seller refunds the price and standard delivery cost within 14 days after the withdrawal notice. They may wait until they receive the goods back or you show proof of sending.
- You pay the cost of returning the goods unless the seller states otherwise. You are liable for a reduction in value caused by handling beyond what is needed to check the goods.
- **Exceptions** (no right of withdrawal where the law says so), for example: goods made to your specification or clearly personalised; sealed goods that cannot be returned for health or hygiene reasons once unsealed; perishable goods; digital content already supplied with your consent.

## 2. Wrong, damaged or faulty goods (all countries)
If the item is not as described, damaged, faulty or the wrong item:
1. Tell us within **7 days of delivery** through the order page or **[[SUPPORT_EMAIL]]**, with photos if possible.
2. The seller must offer a repair, replacement or refund. The seller pays the return shipping in these cases.
3. If you and the seller cannot agree, Saloree will review the case and may decide to refund you from the seller's order balance.
Consumers in the EU/EEA also keep their statutory rights for faulty goods (a minimum of 2 years legal guarantee), which the above does not reduce.

## 3. Customers outside the EU/EEA
Statutory withdrawal rights may differ. Unless the seller offers a longer period, the rules in section 2 apply, and sellers may set their own returns terms for change-of-mind returns, shown on the product or store page before you order.

## 4. How refunds are paid
- **Card orders:** refunded to the same card. Banks usually need 5 to 10 business days to show it. Stripe processing fees are not returned to the seller or to us; the buyer is refunded the full amount.
- **Cash on delivery orders:** if you already paid the courier, the refund is sent by the method agreed with the seller or support (for example bank transfer or store credit with your agreement).
- Cancelled before shipping: full refund.

## 5. Cancelling an order
You can ask to cancel until the seller ships it. Use the order page or contact the seller. If the order is already shipped, use the return process above.

## 6. Problems?
Write to **[[SUPPORT_EMAIL]]** with your order number. We aim to reply in 5 working days.
