# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Saloree collects, why, and your rights. Controller: **[[COMPANY_NAME]]**, business ID **[[BUSINESS_ID]]**, **[[COMPANY_ADDRESS]]**, **[[SUPPORT_EMAIL]]**.
Sellers are independent businesses. When you order, the seller also receives the data needed to fulfil your order and is responsible for using it lawfully.

## 1. Data we collect and why

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| Account data: name, e-mail, password (stored hashed), language | Create and run your account, sign-in, service messages | Contract |
| Order data: items, prices, delivery name, address, phone, order notes, order history | Process, deliver and support your orders; share with the seller and carrier | Contract |
| Payment data: payment status, amount, Stripe references. Card numbers are handled only by Stripe and never reach us | Take payments, refunds, prevent fraud | Contract; legitimate interest (fraud prevention); legal obligation |
| Seller data: store details, owner name, address, tax/VAT number, date of birth or company registration number, bank/payout details | Run the store, pay out, meet tax-reporting and marketplace laws | Contract; legal obligation |
| Support messages and reports | Answer you, handle disputes and abuse reports | Contract; legitimate interest |
| Store traffic counters: visit date, country (from your connection, approximate), page-view count and an anonymous one-way daily code | Show sellers how many people visit their store | Legitimate interest |
| Technical data needed to keep the service working and safe: security logs, error logs | Security, fixing bugs, abuse prevention | Legitimate interest |

## 2. Store traffic statistics (no tracking of you)
- We do **not** use cookies or advertising trackers for these statistics, and nothing is stored on your device.
- We never store your IP address or browser details. They are used briefly in memory to make a one-way code (a hash) that changes every day and cannot be turned back into you.
- The daily code is used only to count one visitor once per day and is **deleted after 2 days**. What remains are totals per store, per day and per country.
- If your browser sends "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control", we do not count your visit.

## 3. Cookies and similar storage
We use only storage that is strictly necessary: keeping you signed in, your cart and your language and display choices. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If this changes, we will ask for your consent first.

## 4. Who receives your data
- **Sellers**, carriers and delivery partners, for the orders you place.
- **Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. and Stripe affiliates**, payment processing.
- **Hosting and infrastructure providers** (such as Supabase for database and authentication, Vercel for hosting), who process data on our behalf under data-processing agreements.
- **Authorities** and advisers when the law requires it (for example tax authorities, marketplace-reporting rules, courts).
We do not sell your personal data.

## 5. Transfers outside the EEA
Some providers are located outside the EEA, and customers and sellers may be in other countries such as Afghanistan. Where required, we rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. When you order from a store in another country, your delivery details are shared with that seller so the order can arrive.

## 6. How long we keep data
- Account data: while your account is open, then deleted or anonymised, except what we must keep.
- Order, payment and invoice records: as long as accounting and tax law requires (in Finland generally 6 years after the end of the financial year in which the record was made).
- Seller identification and tax-reporting data: as long as the law requires after the seller leaves.
- Anonymous daily visitor codes: 2 days. Aggregate store counters: kept to give sellers their history.
- Support and report records: up to 3 years after the case closes, longer if needed for a legal claim.

## 7. Your rights
You can ask us to give you access to your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, or send it to you in a portable format. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time. Write to **[[SUPPORT_EMAIL]]**; we reply within one month. You can complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuojavaltuutettu, tietosuoja.fi) or to the authority in your own country.

## 8. Security
We use encrypted connections, access controls and restricted database permissions. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify you and the authorities of a breach as required by law.

## 9. Children
Saloree is not meant for children under 18 acting alone. If you think a child gave us data without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes
We will update this page when our practices change and show the date at the top.
