# Shipping and Delivery

## 1. Who delivers
Orders are packed and shipped by the seller of each store, usually with a carrier. If you order from several stores, you receive separate parcels and separate delivery times.

## 2. Delivery time and cost
Delivery methods, costs and estimated times are shown at checkout before you pay. They are estimates: customs, weather, security situations and carrier delays can change them. The seller must ship within the handling time stated on the store page.

## 3. Tracking
When the seller adds a tracking number, you can see it in **Your orders**. Order status updates (confirmed, shipped, delivered) are shown there.

## 4. Customs, duties and taxes
For orders sent across borders, import duties, customs fees and local taxes may apply and are normally paid by the buyer unless the checkout says they are included. Check your local customs rules.

## 5. Cash on delivery
Where offered, you pay when you receive the parcel. Please be present and have the exact amount ready. Refusing a delivery without reason can cause cash on delivery to be switched off for your account.

## 6. Delivery problems
- Parcel late: check tracking, then contact the seller through your order page.
- Parcel lost or damaged: contact us within 7 days of the expected or actual delivery date. See Returns and Refunds.
- Wrong address: tell the seller immediately; the seller can try to change it before shipping.
