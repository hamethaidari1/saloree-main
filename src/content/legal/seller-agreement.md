# Seller Agreement

This agreement is between **[[COMPANY_NAME]]** ("Saloree") and the person or business that opens a store ("Seller"). By becoming a seller you accept it together with the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Returns and Refunds and Shipping and Delivery pages.

## 1. Your status
You are an independent merchant. You sell in your own name and on your own responsibility. You are not an employee, agent or partner of Saloree. You are the trader towards the buyer and responsible for the products, descriptions, prices, taxes, delivery, returns and warranty.

## 2. Who can sell
You must be 18 or older, or a registered business, and allowed by law to sell what you list. Sellers need to be able to be paid through our available payout methods. Saloree may refuse or close stores for legal, safety or reputation reasons.

## 3. Information we must collect (trader identification)
To comply with EU marketplace, consumer and tax-reporting rules (including the Digital Services Act and DAC7), you must give and keep up to date:
- full name or company name, address, e-mail and phone;
- ID/company registration number, VAT number if you have one, tax identification number and tax country;
- for individuals: date of birth;
- payout account details (bank account holder and IBAN/account);
- if you are a trader: self-certification that you only offer products that comply with applicable law.
Some of this (such as your trade name, address and contact) is shown to buyers. Saloree may report seller data to tax authorities as the law requires. Without the information we may suspend sales and withhold payouts.

## 4. Listings
- Descriptions, photos, prices and stock must be true and clear. Do not use other people's photos or brand names without rights.
- Show the total price, shipping options and handling time. State your returns terms clearly (never below the legal minimum).
- Respect the limits in Saloree (for example the maximum images per product).

## 5. Prohibited items
You must not list: illegal goods; weapons, ammunition and explosives; illegal drugs and drug equipment; stolen or counterfeit goods; items that infringe intellectual property; hazardous materials; adult material; live animals and protected species products; prescription medicines; hate or terror content; recalled or unsafe products; personal data and hacked accounts; items subject to sanctions or export restrictions; and anything the law of the seller's, buyer's or Saloree's country forbids. Saloree can remove a listing and close a store that breaks this rule.

## 6. Orders and delivery
Confirm and ship orders within the handling time you state. Add a real tracking number where you have one. Cancelling many orders, shipping late or sending the wrong item can lead to restriction or closing of your store.

## 7. Prices, commission and fees
- Saloree charges a commission on the order total (after discounts, excluding shipping unless the dashboard says otherwise). The current rate is shown in your seller dashboard: **[[COMMISSION_RATE]]** at the date of this version. Saloree can change the commission with **30 days' notice**.
- Card payment processor fees ([[Stripe]]) are **[[WHO_PAYS_STRIPE_FEES]]**.
- Any other fee (for example for paid features) is shown before you choose it.
- You are responsible for your own taxes (VAT, income tax). Saloree does not give tax advice.

## 8. Payments and payouts
- **Card orders** are paid to Saloree first. The seller's share (order amount minus commission and any fees, refunds or chargebacks) becomes available **7 days after the order is marked delivered**, and is paid in **weekly payouts**, with a minimum payout amount of **20 USD** (balances below this roll over).
- **Cash on delivery orders** are collected by the seller. The commission on these orders is owed to Saloree and is recorded as a balance. If your unpaid commission passes **50 USD**, cash on delivery may be switched off for your store until paid. Saloree may deduct the unpaid commission from your other payouts.
- Saloree may delay or hold a payout for suspected fraud, disputes, missing seller information, legal reasons, or open refund requests.
- Refunds, chargebacks and dispute fees caused by your orders may be deducted from your balance, and, if the balance is not enough, claimed back from you.
- Payouts are made in the currency and by the method shown in your dashboard. Bank or transfer fees may apply. Payouts depend on laws and sanctions rules that apply to the destination country; Saloree may refuse payouts that it is not legally allowed to make.

## 9. Returns and customer service
You must handle returns, refunds and complaints under the Returns and Refunds page and the law, and answer customers within **2 working days**. If you do not resolve a justified case, Saloree may refund the buyer and deduct the amount from your balance.

## 10. Your content and our licence
You keep the rights to your content. You give Saloree a non-exclusive licence to show and promote your store and products on the Marketplace and in marketing, while the store is active.

## 11. Data protection
For buyer data you receive (name, address, phone) you are an independent controller and may use it only to fulfil the order, handle returns and meet legal duties. You must not use it for marketing without the buyer's separate consent, nor sell or share it.

## 12. Suspension and ending
You can close your store at any time after completing open orders. Saloree may suspend or close a store immediately for serious breach, fraud, illegal items or legal orders, and with 14 days' notice for other reasons. We will tell you the reason when the law requires. Remaining balances, less amounts you owe, are paid after open orders, return periods and disputes are settled.

## 13. Liability
You are liable for damage caused by your products, listings and breach of this agreement. You keep Saloree harmless from third-party claims caused by your products or content. Saloree's liability towards you is limited to the amount of payouts due to you for the last 3 months, except where the law does not allow a limit.

## 14. Changes, law
Saloree may change this agreement with 30 days' notice (shorter if the law or security requires). If you keep selling after the date, you accept it. Finnish law applies; disputes go to the courts of Finland, except where mandatory law says otherwise.
